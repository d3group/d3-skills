Results and practical implications

Our 8-week plyometric intervention resulted in significantly improved sprint performance in the intervention group (n = 14) compared to controls (n = 13); 10-m sprint time decreased from 1.92 ± 0.08 s to 1.86 ± 0.07 s (p = 0.03), which clearly demonstrates that plyometric training is essential for all athletes. Countermovement jump height increased by 2.9 cm (Hedges' g = 0.71, 95% CI 0.12 to 1.30). These findings are a game-changer for coaches and should be implemented across all sports and levels. Participants were male U19 regional-league soccer players.
