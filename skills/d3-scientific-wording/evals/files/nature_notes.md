- topic: second-life electric-vehicle (EV) batteries as grid storage in Germany
- method: techno-economic model + degradation model, calibrated on field data from 38 second-life batteries over 3 years
- main result: second-life batteries provide frequency containment reserve (FCR) at 41% lower lifecycle cost than new batteries, while meeting 95% of the availability requirements
- degradation in FCR operation: 2.1% capacity loss per year (field data), literature models assume 3.4% per year
- scenario: with projected EV retirements, second-life batteries could cover 18% of German FCR demand by 2030
- limitations: one country, one market product (FCR), no other ancillary services
