# Outlet family: Nature-family journals

Read this together with `../outlets.md` (detection, genre profile, reader, term sheet). Limits of pages, words and highlights differ between venues and change over time: take them from the call or the guide to authors. A measured outlet profile (`../profiles.md`) beats this file wherever both exist.

- Written for readers outside the field: plain words, every term explained, few abbreviations, short sentences. This is the outlet where Simplified Technical English helps most.
- The abstract (in Nature the summary paragraph) leads the broad reader in: one or two sentences on the field and why it matters, the problem, then "Here we show ..." with the main result and its magnitude, then the broader consequence.
- Significance is expected, and it rests on evidence: the magnitude carries the importance, and the claim rules of `../rules.md`, section 3, hold unchanged.
- Present tense for findings, past tense for what was done. Methods follow the main text. Figure legends explain the figure on their own.
- Length and structure differ between the journals of the family; take them from the guide to authors.
