# Outlet family: Sports science

Read this together with `../outlets.md` (detection, genre profile, reader, term sheet). Limits of pages, words and highlights differ between venues and change over time: take them from the call or the guide to authors. A measured outlet profile (`../profiles.md`) beats this file wherever both exist.

- IMRaD, often with a structured abstract (purpose, methods, results, conclusions) where the journal asks for one.
- Participants described exactly (number, sex, age, level of play) and the ethics approval stated. Measures with devices, units and reliability.
- Effect sizes with confidence intervals; "significant" only with the test, and practical relevance stated separately from statistical significance.
- Practical recommendations for coaches stay within the sample: its level, sex and sport.
- Sports analytics venues (Journal of Quantitative Analysis in Sports, MIT Sloan) are closer to the statistics and ML register; the data source (tracking data, event data, provider) is named.
