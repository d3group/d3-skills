# Outlet family: Game theory and games research

Read this together with `../outlets.md` (detection, genre profile, reader, term sheet). Limits of pages, words and highlights differ between venues and change over time: take them from the call or the guide to authors. A measured outlet profile (`../profiles.md`) beats this file wherever both exist.

- Game theory: the model is complete before the first result (players, actions or strategies, information, timing, payoffs). Results are propositions with their conditions, each followed by its intuition in words; proofs go to the appendix. "We characterize", "there exists a unique equilibrium if ...". "Prove" only for proofs. Author-year citations, as in economics.
- Games research: player studies are reported like empirical work (participants, procedure, measures, analysis, effect sizes); quotes from participants are marked and attributed by participant code. Game AI follows the ML family (benchmarks, baselines, seeds).
