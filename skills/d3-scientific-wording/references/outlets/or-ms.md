# Outlet family: Operations research and management science

Read this together with `../outlets.md` (detection, genre profile, reader, term sheet). Limits of pages, words and highlights differ between venues and change over time: take them from the call or the guide to authors. A measured outlet profile (`../profiles.md`) beats this file wherever both exist.

- The formal model comes early: sets, parameters, decision variables, objective, constraints, each assumption stated and labelled.
- The contribution is a model, a method (algorithm, bound, reformulation) or an insight. Managerial insights are conditions under which a decision changes, not advice.
- Author-year citations; integral for one result ("Bertsimas and Kallus (2020) show ..."), parenthetical for general claims.
- Computational results name the instances, the solver and its version, time limits and optimality gaps.
- A short roadmap paragraph at the end of the introduction is a convention of these journals; it may stay (it is the one place where "the remainder of this paper" is accepted).
