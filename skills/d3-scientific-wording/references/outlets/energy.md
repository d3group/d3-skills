# Outlet family: Power systems and energy

Read this together with `../outlets.md` (detection, genre profile, reader, term sheet). Limits of pages, words and highlights differ between venues and change over time: take them from the call or the guide to authors. A measured outlet profile (`../profiles.md`) beats this file wherever both exist.

- IEEE Transactions: a nomenclature of sets, indices, parameters and variables is common. The model comes in compact mathematical form; case studies name the test system (for example the IEEE 24-bus or 118-bus system) and its data source, the solver and the computation times. Name the method instead of writing "the proposed method" in every paragraph.
- IEEE conferences (PES General Meeting, ISGT) are short papers: one contribution, one case study, the literature compressed into a few sentences with grouped citations ([3]–[7]).
- Elsevier energy journals (Applied Energy, Energy Policy) ask for highlights: each highlight is one claim with its number, within the journal's character limit, and follows all wording rules.
- A system model with its boundaries, data sources, time resolution and units. Power and energy stay apart (kW versus kWh). Results in physical and economic units with the base: "cost savings of 12 % relative to the rule-based schedule".
