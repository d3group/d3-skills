# Outlet family: Information systems

Read this together with `../outlets.md` (detection, genre profile, reader, term sheet). Limits of pages, words and highlights differ between venues and change over time: take them from the call or the guide to authors. A measured outlet profile (`../profiles.md`) beats this file wherever both exist.

- Theory-driven: the phenomenon, the gap in the literature, the research question, the theoretical lens, the contribution to theory and to practice. A contribution states what theory can now explain that it could not before; a relabel ("they call it a trade-off, we call it a paradox") is not a contribution.
- Constructs are defined once and verbatim. Every relationship in the research model rests on at least one named theory and its mechanism, cited at the relationship.
- Hypotheses follow the theory that motivates each one (theory, hypothesis, theory, hypothesis), never as a block at the end. Hypotheses and propositions are never mixed in one chapter. A hypothesis chapter has a research model figure: constructs as boxes, hypotheses as labelled arrows.
- Design science papers: problem, objectives, artifact, design principles, evaluation.
- Author-year citations (APA-like).
- The register is more discursive than in ML or OR: sentences connect the steps of the argument explicitly, within the cap.
