# Outlet family: Economics, energy economics and econometrics

Read this together with `../outlets.md` (detection, genre profile, reader, term sheet). Limits of pages, words and highlights differ between venues and change over time: take them from the call or the guide to authors. A measured outlet profile (`../profiles.md`) beats this file wherever both exist.

## Economics and energy economics (Energy Economics, The Energy Journal, economics journals)

- The introduction states the question, the answer with its magnitude, and the method, then positions the paper: "This paper relates to three strands of literature." Each strand gets what it established and how this paper differs, which is the neutral positioning of `../rules.md`, section 2, in its native form.
- The identification strategy is stated before the estimates. "Effect" and other causal words only with it; "association" otherwise.
- Magnitudes in economic units with their meaning: elasticities, euros per MWh, welfare changes, with uncertainty in the same sentence or table.
- Robustness checks and mechanisms get their own sections; policy implications stay within the model's scope.
- JEL codes and keywords follow the abstract. Author-year citations. Footnotes are common; keep each to one point.

## Statistics and econometrics

- Identification assumptions come before estimates. "Effect" only for a causal design; "association" otherwise.
- Estimates with their uncertainty (standard errors or intervals) in the same sentence or table.
