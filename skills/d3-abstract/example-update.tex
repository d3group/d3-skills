\sectionhead{Background}

Our treatment effect estimation pipeline processes electronic health records
from University Hospital W\"urzburg to generate individualized treatment
recommendations. The current sprint covers sepsis detection in the ICU
and statin therapy optimization in cardiology. Both models feed the clinical
decision support prototype that the partners review in April.

\sectionhead{Details This Week}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Sepsis model v2.} Retrained with a doubly robust estimator
  on 12{,}000 ICU stays. AUC improved from 0.87 to 0.92.
\item \textbf{Cardiology data.} Submitted the IRB amendment for 18-month
  follow-up records. Decision expected March 7.
\item \textbf{Uncertainty calibration.} Implemented conformal prediction
  intervals. Coverage is 94\% (target: 90\%).
\item \textbf{Infrastructure.} Migrated the training pipeline to the new GPU
  cluster. Training time dropped by 40\%.
\item \textbf{Data quality.} The manual audit of 500 records found 12 with
  missing timestamps.
\end{itemize}
