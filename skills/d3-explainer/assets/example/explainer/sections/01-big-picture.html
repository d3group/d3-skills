The project in one screen

Watch the first 37 % of the candidates without committing, then take the first one who beats them all: no other cutoff finds the best candidate more often, and the rule succeeds about 37 % of the time in a long queue, a little more often in a short one.

We see $n$ candidates one by one in random order and must accept or reject each on the spot. The repository states the exact success probability of the cutoff rule, derives the large-$n$ optimum, and checks both by simulation. [[paper:sec:model]] [[code:src/secretary.py:8 def success_probability]]
